Who runs this spreadsheet

kakobuy spreadsheet is an independently maintained list of 4,000+ KakoBuy replica finds with warehouse QC photos. It started in 2024 as a private haul notebook for Poland, then opened as a public spreadsheet so other buyers could skip dead links and stock photos.

Who writes the pages

Pages are written by the kakobuy spreadsheet editorial team — people who actually order through a shopping agent, wait for warehouse photos, and ship to Europe. We are not KakoBuy staff, we do not run a warehouse, and we do not manufacture the goods on this list.

How a find gets listed

A card is added only when there is a live KakoBuy link and at least one real QC-style photo. Popular finds are re-checked. The homepage “last reviewed” date is 21 September 2026. If a seller link dies, we remove or replace it rather than leave a broken card.

What we will not claim

We do not promise that branded goods will clear EU customs. We do not call replicas “authentic”. We do not hide that this is an affiliate site: if you register or order through a KakoBuy link here, we may earn a small commission. Browsing stays free.
